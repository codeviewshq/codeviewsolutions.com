## Users and purpose

CodeView Solutions is a website and digital product services company with consulting work. It helps businesses plan and create websites, portals, products, and integrations using APIs or third-party products. The established audience is small and midsize businesses; no narrower industry or new audience has been confirmed.

The October 2026 positioning change replaces the former website-care offer. Shared content in `src/data/site.js` follows this product record:

1. Websites: create or improve a business's web presence.
2. Portals and digital products: design and build tools around a business need.
3. Integrations: connect systems using APIs and third-party products.
4. Consulting: help clarify the problem, evaluate options, and decide what to build or connect.

Success is an enquiry for a free consultation leading to an agreed engagement. Home introduces the broader offer; Services explains the work; Work provides clearly labeled placeholders; About explains the approach; Contact offers a free consultation. Keep product demo placeholders visible, with working demos deferred until material is ready.

## Confirmed offer

- A free consultation is the primary next step.
- No subscription plans or published plan prices. Remove Care, Improve, Grow, billing controls, and plan-specific enquiry context from the redesigned site.
- Scope, fees, timelines, revisions, hosting and third-party costs, support, and warranty terms are agreed per engagement. No replacement rates, delivery promises, or fixed inclusions have been confirmed.
- The former free-first-design and fixed website-care package claims must not be carried into the broader offer without confirmation.
- Client retains ownership and control of the website, domain, hosting accounts, code, content, and documentation.
- Initial reply within one business day. This is not a completion deadline or 24/7 support promise.

Do not invent pricing, consultation scheduling availability, named supported products, product capabilities, or contractual terms.

## Brand and evidence

- The October 2026 redesign replaces Redline. Preserve the supplied logo from `first.jpg` and use it to guide the new identity. Support light and dark themes.
- The voice and experience should be subtle, calm, and inviting. Copy reads like a person talking: plain, complete sentences that name a problem the visitor recognizes (a dated website, copying data between tools, work outgrowing spreadsheets, not knowing what to do next) and then say what we do about it. The owner rejected slogan-style fragments and one-word marketing ("Useful work. A clear purpose."); don't write headlines like that. Avoid the old website-care positioning and sales-heavy language.
- Lead with the business value and a coherent company point of view; do not simply repeat the owner's implementation categories as the hero message. The owner delegates creative direction and requests one implemented design to refine, rather than further variant rounds.
- Include purposeful motion graphics, animated diagrams, and a considered background. Product demos can follow later. Use clearly labeled placeholders on the work page for now.
- Use “we” and describe what the company delivers. Do not imply headcount, add staff photos, founder bios, or solo/freelance framing.
- Real delivered work cannot be named because of NDAs or former-employer ownership. Do not invent client names, logos, testimonials, metrics, certifications, awards, years of experience, or years in business.
- Work and product demos use clearly labeled placeholders for now. Do not fabricate case studies, concept projects, working demos, customer data, or results to fill them.
- The site's own craft is the current demonstrable portfolio piece.

## Product principles

1. Explain outcomes and responsibilities in plain language.
2. Start with a free consultation and agree scope and commercial terms before work starts.
3. Claim only what can be shown.
4. Keep the client in control of the outcome and accounts.
5. Make enquiry routes work before offering them.
6. Preserve keyboard access, visible focus, sufficient contrast, reduced motion, and layouts from 320px upward.
