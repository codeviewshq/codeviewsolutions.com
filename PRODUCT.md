# Product

## Users and purpose

CodeView Solutions helps small and midsize businesses improve and look after their websites. The buyer is usually a non-technical owner or operations lead who needs clear outcomes, costs, and responsibility.

The current offer in `src/data/site.js` is authoritative:

1. Modern Website Refresh: improve design, content, navigation, and the customer experience.
2. Ongoing Website Care: agreed updates, monitoring, fixes, and planned improvements.
3. Reliable Growth Infrastructure: hosting reliability, capacity planning, and business connections.

Success is a real enquiry leading to an agreed engagement. Home explains the offer and prices; Services describes the work; About shows the approach and an explicitly illustrative review; Contact provides the next step.

## Confirmed offer

- Care: $10/month or $90/year.
- Improve: $50/month or $540/year.
- Grow: $100/month or $1,080/year.
- Free consultation and first website design within an agreed scope.
- Before work starts, agree design scope/revisions, monthly update allowance, hosting and third-party costs, and cancellation terms. These details are not settled; do not invent them.
- Extra work is quoted separately.
- Refreshes include a 90-day warranty on agreed work. New work is separate.
- Client retains ownership and control of the website, domain, hosting accounts, code, content, and documentation.
- Initial reply within one business day. This is not a completion deadline or 24/7 support promise.

Prices live only in `plans` in `src/data/site.js`; page markup reads them rather than retyping figures.

## Contact and technical constraints

The public phone and hello@codeviewsolutions.com email are configured. The HTTPS form endpoint remains `<VARIABLE>` while the owner builds a custom solution. The homepage hero and Contact page show the form design with submission disabled and an email alternative until a real HTTPS receiver is set. Unconfigured links are hidden. Do not promise online delivery while unconfigured.

Astro 5 static output, five pages, shared shell, native form POST when configured. No database or server runtime. Node.js 22 is the documented build environment. Browser layout, accessibility, contrast, reduced-motion, billing, and enquiry-context checks run before deployment.

Work is remote across the US. No town, state, or postal address is published by owner decision.

## Brand and evidence

- Redline is the only design: drafting stock, navy from the supplied logo, revision red, and graphite. Preserve the logo from `first.jpg` and the visual identity in `DESIGN.md`.
- Use “we” and describe what the company delivers. Do not imply headcount, add staff photos, founder bios, or solo/freelance framing.
- Real delivered work cannot be named because of NDAs or former-employer ownership. Do not invent client names, logos, testimonials, metrics, certifications, awards, years of experience, or years in business.
- The sample review is a hypothetical demonstration of the approach. Keep that label prominent; it is not evidence of a client outcome.
- The site's own craft is the current demonstrable portfolio piece.

## Product principles

1. Explain outcomes and responsibilities in plain language.
2. Publish the confirmed plan prices and agree limits before work starts.
3. Claim only what can be shown.
4. Keep the client in control of the outcome and accounts.
5. Make enquiry routes work before offering them.
6. Preserve keyboard access, visible focus, sufficient contrast, reduced motion, and layouts from 320px upward.
